💬 Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions from Finance & Founder Clients

How does it catch duplicate charges or billing spikes?

Every new transaction is checked against recent history for matching amounts and merchants, instantly flagging likely duplicates for a quick human decision.

Does this integrate with QuickBooks Online or Xero?

Yes. Once transactions are reconciled, results can sync into QuickBooks, Xero, or a database of your choice via a standard integration.

Can I ask conversational questions about my financials?

Yes. A built-in assistant lets founders and accountants chat directly with their ledger - e.g. "Compare marketing expense vs revenue last month."

How is financial data protected?

Financial records are encrypted at rest and in transit, with access limited to only what each part of the system strictly needs.
