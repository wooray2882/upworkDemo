💬 Frequently Asked Questions

Everything Clients Ask Before Starting

Can it handle messy scans, mobile photos, and skewed receipts?

Yes. It's built to handle natural, imperfect images - crumpled receipts, low-resolution phone photos, and multi-page PDFs - without needing pre-cropping.

How long does it take to set this up for my workflow?

The core pipeline is already built and tested. Timeline depends on your specific document types and any custom fields you need - let's discuss your case directly.

Can this connect to QuickBooks, Xero, or my own database?

Yes. Structured results can be pushed via webhook, API, or a direct database connection to whatever system you already use.

What will this cost to run monthly?

It's usage-based, not a flat SaaS subscription - you pay for what you actually process. Exact numbers depend on your volume; happy to work through it together.
