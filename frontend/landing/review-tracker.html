💬 Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions from Product & SaaS Clients

How does aspect-based sentiment differ from regular star ratings?

A 3-star review might say "Love the fast dashboard, but pricing is confusing and support didn't reply." This decomposes that into distinct aspect scores instead of averaging them into one number.

Can we connect this to our internal Slack or Discord?

Yes. Churn alerts can be formatted and pushed directly into any designated channel with customer info and suggested next steps.

Can it summarize hundreds of reviews into an executive report?

Yes. A summary step can turn a month's feedback into a short, bulleted report ranking top complaints and feature requests.

How do we backfill existing historical reviews?

You can upload a CSV or JSON export of your existing reviews, and they'll be processed in batches alongside new incoming feedback.
