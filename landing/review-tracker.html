💬 Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions from Product & SaaS Clients

How does aspect-based sentiment differ from regular star ratings?

A 3-star review might say "Love the fast dashboard, but pricing is confusing and support didn't reply." Our model decomposes this into 3 distinct aspect scores rather than averaging them out.

Can we connect this to our internal Slack or Discord?

Yes. AWS SNS topics format incoming churn alerts and push them directly into any designated Slack channel with customer info and pre-drafted response actions.

Can it summarize 500 reviews into an executive report?

Yes. A Bedrock synthesis state aggregates the entire month's feedback into a bulleted 1-page executive summary ranking top user complaints and feature requests.

How do we backfill existing historical reviews?

You can drop a single CSV or JSON dump into the S3 bucket; Step Functions Distributed Map processes 10,000 historical reviews in parallel in under 3 minutes.
