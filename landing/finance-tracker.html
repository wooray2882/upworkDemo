💬 Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions from Finance & Founder Clients

How does it catch duplicate charges or billing spikes?

A dedicated validation Lambda runs fuzzy matching against existing DynamoDB transactions within a rolling 72-hour window, instantly flagging duplicate amounts from the same merchant.

Does this integrate with QuickBooks Online or Xero?

Yes. Once transactions are reconciled, an EventBridge target can trigger bidirectional sync into QuickBooks, Xero, or PostgreSQL via standard OAuth2 REST APIs.

Can I ask conversational questions about my financials?

Yes. The built-in Bedrock Knowledge Base vector store allows founders and accountants to chat directly with their ledger (e.g. "Compare marketing expense vs revenue last month").

How is financial data protected?

All financial records are encrypted at rest with AWS KMS and in transit with TLS 1.3. The pipeline runs entirely inside your own AWS VPC with strict IAM least-privilege policies.
