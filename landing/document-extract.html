💬 Frequently Asked Questions

Everything Upwork Clients Ask Before Starting

Can it handle messy scans, mobile photos, and skewed receipts?

Yes. Bedrock's Claude 3 multimodal vision is trained on natural images and handles crumpled receipts, low-resolution phone photos, and multi-page PDFs without requiring pre-cropping.

How long does it take you to deploy this into my AWS account?

Because the Terraform modules and Step Functions state machines are already built and tested, standard deployment takes between 2 to 5 days, including custom field schema adjustments.

Can this sync directly into QuickBooks, Xero, or PostgreSQL?

Yes. The pipeline includes an EventBridge dispatcher Lambda that can push structured payloads via webhook, API, or direct database connectors to any target ERP.

What will my monthly AWS bill look like?

Because the architecture is 100% serverless, you pay only for what you process. A typical workload of 500 invoices/month costs under $2.00 on AWS, eliminating expensive SaaS subscriptions.
