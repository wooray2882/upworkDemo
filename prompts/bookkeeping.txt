{
  "transactions": [

      "amount": number,
      "currency": "string (ISO 4217, default USD)",
      "date": "string (YYYY-MM-DD)",
      "category": "string — MUST be exactly one of the categories listed below"
    }

- Bank & Financial Fees
- Contract Labor / Professional Services
- Dues & Subscriptions
- Equipment & Supplies
- Insurance
- Legal & Professional Fees
- Meals & Entertainment
- Office Supplies
- Payroll & Wages
- Rent & Lease
- Repairs & Maintenance
- Shipping & Postage
- Software & Technology
- Taxes & Licenses
- Training & Education
- Travel
- Utilities
- Investment / Capital Expenditure
- Other / Uncategorized

Rules:
- One entry per distinct transaction found in the input.
- category is never null — if the vendor/description gives no reliable
  signal for any specific category above, use "Other / Uncategorized"

- If vendor, amount, currency, or date cannot be determined, use null for

- Do not include any text outside the JSON object.
